Opening stock

What was on the shelves on the opening date, item by item, with the unit cost. The Stores Officer enters it (many items at once, or from a spreadsheet); the Finance Manager checks it and posts it. Posting puts the stock in and records its value in the books (Inventory against Owner's capital). An item that already has stock movements cannot get opening stock: correct it with a stock adjustment.

Enter opening stock

The day the shelves were counted. It cannot be in the future, and its month must still be open in the books.

Paste or upload a list (optional)
