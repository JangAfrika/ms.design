Social security rates

Percent of each employee's taxable pay. Enter the rates from the Social Security and Housing Finance Corporation. The system does not assume any rate.

How PAYE is worked out

PAYE is charged on monthly taxable pay band by band: each band's rate applies only to the part of the pay inside that band. Enter the GRA bands with the date they apply from; when the GRA changes the bands, add the new set with its new start date (older payrolls keep the old bands).

Example: bands 0–1,000 at 0%, 1,000–3,000 at 10%, above 3,000 at 20%. Taxable pay 5,000 → 0 + 200 + 400 = GMD 400.
