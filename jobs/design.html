Design

Design work

Assign designer

The designer is told about the job. The due time is when the first proof should be ready.

Design brief

What the customer wants. Saving a brief opens a design task for the job if there is none.

Upload a design version

The files are saved in the job’s Design folder on Google Drive with the standard file name. Earlier versions that are still open are marked as replaced.

Internal review

Sent to customer

Mark the approved proof as sent. The job moves to Customer Approval.

Customer decision

Approval needs evidence: the signed proof, the email, or a WhatsApp screenshot.

Log a revision request

Use this when a change is asked for outside a customer decision, for example by Production. The approved version is set aside and the design goes back to work.
