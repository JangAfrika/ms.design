Account

Codes have 4 digits: 1 assets, 2 liabilities, 3 equity, 4 revenue, 5 cost of sales, 6 expenses. A heading groups accounts and takes no postings. Once an account has postings its code and type stay fixed.

Manual journal

For corrections, opening balances, depreciation and other entries that no document posts. Debits must equal credits. It is posted at once and can only be undone by a reversal.
